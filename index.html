What it does

  • Splits a C printf format into its parts and shows what each flag, width, precision and length modifier means.
  • Prints the result with spaces made visible, including the effect of * arguments, truncation by hh and h, and negative numbers as unsigned.
  • Flags the combinations the C standard leaves undefined, and missing or extra arguments.

How far to trust it

  • On 34,318 generated formats it agrees with gcc 11.4 and glibc printf on every case the standard defines (13,852). It does not compute %f, %e, %g, %p or %n. The rules follow the cppreference fprintf page, which was fetched.
